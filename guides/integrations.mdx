## Connecting WhatsApp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the WhatsApp integration page">
    In your Mais Linda dashboard, navigate to **Integrations** → **WhatsApp**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your WhatsApp Business account">
    Click **Connect Account** and follow the prompts to authorize your WhatsApp Business account through Meta's verification flow. You'll need admin access to your Meta Business Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your agent">
    Choose the agent you want to handle incoming WhatsApp messages. All messages sent to your connected business number will be routed to this agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send a test message">
    Send a WhatsApp message from a personal number to your business number and confirm that your agent replies correctly. Check the **Conversations** tab in your dashboard to review the exchange.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Connecting Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Slack integration page">
    In your Mais Linda dashboard, navigate to **Integrations** → **Slack**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the Slack app">
    Click **Add to Slack** and authorize the installation in your Slack workspace. You'll need permission to install apps in your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your agent">
    Choose the agent that will respond to messages. You can configure whether the agent responds to direct messages, mentions in channels, or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test in Slack">
    Send a direct message to the Mais Linda bot in Slack or mention it in a channel with `@MaisLinda` and confirm it responds as expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## CRM & Helpdesk Integrations

Mais Linda doesn't offer native plugins for every CRM and helpdesk tool, but you can push conversation data to any platform — including **Salesforce**, **HubSpot**, and **Zendesk** — using webhooks.

The general pattern is:

1. **Register a webhook** on your Mais Linda account for the events you care about (e.g., `conversation.started`, `message.received`, `conversation.closed`).
2. **Receive the payload** at your server endpoint and extract the relevant data (thread ID, user ID, message content, timestamp).
3. **Push the data** to your CRM or helpdesk via their API — create a contact, open a ticket, or log the conversation.

This approach gives you full control over what gets synced and how it's mapped to your existing data model. For a complete walkthrough of webhook setup and payload verification, see the [Webhooks](/guides/webhooks) guide.

## Building a Custom Integration

Any platform or interface can be connected to a Mais Linda agent using the chat API directly. This is the recommended approach for mobile apps, interactive voice response (IVR) systems, or any channel not covered by the built-in integrations.

The pattern is simple:

1. **Receive a message** from a user in your custom interface.
2. **Forward it to Mais Linda** by calling `POST /v1/agents/{agentId}/chat` with the message content and, optionally, a `thread_id` to maintain conversation context.
3. **Return the reply** from the API response back to the user in your interface.

```bash
curl -X POST https://api.maislinda.com/v1/agents/{agentId}/chat \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "message": "Do you offer international shipping?",
    "thread_id": "thread_abc123"
  }'
```

Pass the same `thread_id` across turns to maintain conversation history. Omit it on the first message and use the `thread_id` from the response for all subsequent turns in that session.

Because the chat API accepts any string message, you can pipe input from any source — parsed voice transcripts, form submissions, or messages from a proprietary messaging platform — into a Mais Linda agent and stream the responses back to users in real time.
